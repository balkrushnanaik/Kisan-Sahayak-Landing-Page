About Kisan Sahayak

Technology that connects Farmers, Labourers and Agricultural Services.

A dedicated digital platform designed specifically for rural communities. We simplify the process of finding reliable farm labor, accessing heavy machinery, and trading agricultural products locally, empowering the agricultural backbone of our nation.

Explore the Idea Contact the Founder

Our Mission

"Our mission is to make essential agricultural services easier to find, easier to access, and more transparent for farmers and rural workers."

Understanding the Problem

Traditional farming faces several logistical challenges that hold back productivity and growth.

👨‍🌾

Finding Labour

Difficulty in finding reliable workers when needed most during peak seasons.

🚜

Machinery Access

High costs prevent small farmers from accessing modern agricultural machinery.

🌾

Selling Products

Limited reach to local markets leads to unfair prices for hard-grown produce.

🔗

Scattered Services

No single platform connects all these essential agricultural services efficiently.
